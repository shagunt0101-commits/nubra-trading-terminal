1Flip RSI exit — ride winners LOCK MORE

Today: RSI ≥ 70 → sell market. In a trend, RSI rides >70 for many bars while premium runs — you sell the strongest minute of every winner.

New: RSI ≥ 70 → stay in, keep trailing; RSI < 70 → market exit. Winners extend to the fade, not the pop.

2Broker SL/TP at entry LOSE LESS

Today: entry order is plain ENTRY — SL/TP live only in app memory. Crash or restart = naked position.

New: place as ENTRY_AND_EXIT FLEXI → hard SL + TP sit on the broker. Protection-first.

380%-TP milestone: TP off, SL→BE BOTH

Today: TP hits +4 → app exits, runner dead at the first goal.

New: at 80% of TP (e.g. +3.2 pts) modify FLEXI: disable TP, SL → breakeven. Risk now zero; win runs on the RSI trail.

4Point-buffer trail, not 20% LOCK MORE

20% of ₹2.5 premium = ₹0.5 = pure noise; 20% of ₹10 = ₹2 = too tight. One tick dumps winners on cheap options.

New: absolute buffer max(1.5 pts, 12% of peak) — tight on cheap, proportional on rich. Trails match the instrument.

52-close RSI confirm BOTH

Today: exit on any single RSI <70 print → one-bar dip at 71→68 dumps a runner.

New: exit only after 2 consecutive 1m closes < 70. Whipsaw-proofs the fade exit.
